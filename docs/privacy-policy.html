Privacy Policy for Dispatch

Google Play Store & Meta Compliant • Effective October 12, 2026
Zero-Knowledge Architecture: Dispatch operates with strict local client-side processing. Your spreadsheets, student rosters, phone numbers, and message templates are processed exclusively in your device's volatile memory (RAM) and are NEVER uploaded to any third-party tracking server.

1. Introduction

Dispatch ("the Application") is developed and maintained by independent engineer Saketh Goljana ("Developer", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to transparency and user privacy for all users worldwide.

2. Information We Process

We process the following types of data exclusively on your device:

  • Spreadsheet Records (.xlsx, .xls, .csv): Loaded directly into local smartphone memory to resolve personalization tokens (such as names, class, balance dues). This data is never transmitted to external cloud servers.
  • WhatsApp Meta API Credentials: If you use the Official WhatsApp Business API, your Meta Phone ID and Access Token are stored locally in Android SharedPreferences / Encrypted Device Storage. Calls to Meta Graph API are made directly from your device to official Meta servers (graph.facebook.com) via TLS encryption.
  • Local Campaign History: Delivery statuses and dispatch audit logs are stored offline on your phone and can be deleted by you at any time.

3. Permissions Requested

  • Storage / Document Access (SAF Picker): Required solely to allow you to select and import your spreadsheet files. Dispatch reads only the files you explicitly pick.
  • Internet Connectivity: Required strictly when sending messages via the official Meta Cloud API or opening WhatsApp click-to-chat deep links.

4. Third-Party Services

  • WhatsApp & Meta Platforms, Inc.: Clicking personalized link actions launches the official WhatsApp client on your phone. API calls adhere strictly to the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.
  • No Analytics or Ad Trackers: Dispatch does not integrate Google AdMob, Facebook SDK, Firebase Analytics, or any third-party marketing SDK.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

Active spreadsheet data is automatically cleared from RAM when a campaign completes. You can also wipe all offline cached history and stored API credentials at any time directly in the app via Settings > Legal > Clear Data.
